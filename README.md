## 📌 Project Overview

The **Retail Sales Analytics Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze retail sales performance, revenue trends, customer activity, product performance, regional sales, and sales channel distribution.

This dashboard converts raw sales data into meaningful business insights using **Power BI, DAX, Power Query, and interactive visualizations**.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze overall retail sales performance
- Track total sales and transactions
- Understand customer volume
- Identify top-performing products
- Compare sales across different regions
- Analyze Online vs Offline sales
- Monitor monthly sales trends
- Provide interactive business insights
